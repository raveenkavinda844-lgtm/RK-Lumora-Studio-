The studio philosophy

Some things are too meaningful to lose.

RK Lumora Studio brings together fine art and the careful preservation of cultural objects. From original drawings and portrait commissions to painting conservation and paper-based materials, every project is approached with patience, respect, and attention to detail.

Creativity makes new memories. Conservation helps meaningful ones endure.
